# Research leverage: fast, high-impact, high-citation-potential science (on demand)

**Scope:** WorkBuddy adapter tactics for this user's explicit outcome goal, **not** a second scientific policy or a promise of publications/citations. PD-RW WORKFLOW, HIGH_VALUE_RESEARCH_DESIGN, PAPER_FIRST, VENUE_STRATEGY, original user choices, permissions and Run evidence remain authoritative. Do not pre-read on routine resume.

## Outcome to optimize

Shorten **time to a submission-ready, credible, independently supportable high-impact scientific paper contribution** with a realistic high-tier journal route (Q1 preferred; credible JCR Q2+ minimum under PD-RW) and a plausible path to broad scholarly reuse. Publication/citation prediction is inherently uncertain; never output invented acceptance rates, citation counts or fixed journal ranks. Speed must come from fewer low-value experiments, decisive probes and reusable assets—not weaker baselines, post-hoc testing, premature claims, or writing without authorization.

## New-project H3/H4/H6: three distinct paper-driven author selections

Within the user's H1/H2 scope, investigate a breadth of plausible opportunities (often 6–10 initial ideas, scaled by evidence) before selecting up to three with **different failure premises** for the H3 author card. After H3 author selection, study 2–3 alternative evidence paths for a separate H4 author card; only then construct the H5 draft/H6 specific paper-contribution plan with legal adaptive-pivot scope. Compare candidates in this order:

1. **Importance and audience:** what meaningful unmet scientific/engineering need is answered, who beyond the original dataset can use the result, and why this matters; record contrary evidence.
2. **Concrete novelty gap:** named closest original research, matching legal information/evaluation, and the specific new knowledge/ability if this succeeds. An unaccessed paper stays UNKNOWN.
3. **Scientific identifiability:** independent truth/groups, confounders, legal observation time, stronger simple alternative and a real independent confirmation path. Metadata sufficiency is provisional.
4. **Reuse/citation potential:** plausible mechanisms for others to reuse the result (measurement law, replicable benchmark, general method, reusable code/data when licensed, cross-domain relevance). Treat these as **hypotheses**, not predicted citations or permission to release assets.
5. **Fastest credible route:** smallest legal probe that changes the decision, expected dependency/compute bottleneck and failure-triggered fallback; reuse prior legitimate Run/data where suitable.

At **H3** select the paper-worthy problem, at **H4** select legal independent evidence, at **H6** select the Task, expected contribution and allowed adaptive method/contribution changes. New H6 v3 binds separate H3/H4 choices and has `adaptive_pivot_envelope` in each option. Use existing `recommendation_rationale` and option fields to explain publication value and shortest evidence path. Do not add unofficial fields or silently alter versioned card digests; historical H6 v1/v2 remain unchanged. No extra journal-selection questionnaire.

## Exploration maturity, small contributions and negative evidence

A first legal Pilot is for *learning*, not proving Q2 readiness or ruling out the entire research line. Keep separate: `NOT_RUN/DATA_BLOCKED`, `ENGINEERING_FAILURE/INVALID_EXPERIMENT`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `VALID_NEGATIVE` (specific premise/condition only), and `VALID_POSITIVE` (bounded evidence, not automatically a paper). Unknown truth or a weak preliminary metric is not scientific failure.

A modest independent result, cost/stability boundary, meaningful correction of an existing method, new physical measurement or negative discovery can have a credible Q2+ paper route; neither SOTA nor a novel neural network is mandatory. Compare the actual named nearest neighbor, real application value, evidence independence and minimum confirmation gaps. Do not upgrade an ordinary tuning change or cherry-picked slice into novelty. Conversely, when a credible paper contribution already exists, prioritize its *remaining evidence* over endless speculative algorithm upgrades.

After failure, distinguish lack of information, invalid evaluator, underfitting, a disproven local mechanism and missing novelty; compare a genuinely different legal information lever or task. With no discriminating legal probe, HOLD the branch and offer alternatives, not an automatic whole-project STOP. Reuse prior positive *and* negative Runs with their precise scopes; never infer universal transfer from one dataset.

## Active H7 DEV: maximize information gained per experiment

When authorized, use the least costly *decision-relevant* credible probe and benchmark that can meaningfully distinguish competing explanations; do not automatically choose the lowest raw-cost probe if it cannot change the scientific or paper decision. Use actual errors and group/measurement structure to propose a bounded mechanism. Reuse valid Runs; do not substitute multiple random seeds, architecture swapping or dashboards for evidence. The optional `science.cli decision` response includes `declared_probe_efficiency`: a conservative Pareto comparison of **claimed** hypothesis-pair discrimination and ordinal cost. It does **not** rank scientific merit or authorize a Run.

After a valid negative result, first identify which part failed: data/truth, evaluation, information/identifiability, insufficient training, mechanism, or novelty. An initial new-algorithm proposal is **not** a permanent objective: compare a meaningful older-method improvement, new measurement/reliability boundary, mechanism finding or revised task on actual independent paper value, not whether the original algorithm survived. If another distinct legal information lever is supported, pursue a bounded falsifying probe in current H6 scope; otherwise preserve the valid result and propose a scoped branch NARROW/REFRAME/HOLD, with H8 only for a material core-Claim or strategy decision. Do not automatically STOP the whole project or start a new major line. Expensive experiments need a specified expected change to the science decision and a cost ceiling, not additional paperwork.

## Select the next useful action, not another review

For a material bottleneck, reuse existing results and compare the leading
in-scope route against a genuinely different plausible alternative (or explain
why none is warranted). State the missing premise, strongest competing
explanation, cheapest credible discriminating evidence, and how each possible
outcome changes the next action. If the current evidence already distinguishes
them, act on that evidence rather than repeating the probe. During authorized
ordinary DEV, group related fixes, checks and experiments into one bounded
continuous task; only reopen the affected check after a material change or
new contradiction. Do not require a new card, extra Agent, fixed review round,
or author confirmation for ordinary in-scope work. A valid negative may
support a narrower contribution or a different information lever; an
unverified result is not a reason to claim success or abandon a project.

When a specialist external research system is genuinely needed, suggest the
capability and the minimal evidence/constraint handoff to PD-RW-Orchestrator;
do not reimplement its search or scheduler, invoke an external model by
default, or treat its output as verified evidence. Existing author choices
and protected-effect permissions remain authoritative.

## Fast evidence-to-paper transition (without bypassing writing lock)

At a meaningful positive or negative Run, map supported claims, named nearest work, missing independent evidence and realistic paper type using existing `RESEARCH_RECORD.md` / `PAPER_BLUEPRINT.md`. Separate **scientific result**, **independent contribution**, **journal fit**, and **citation/reuse potential**; do not label any of these CONFIRMED from editorial prose or a hypothetical venue. If evidence is sufficient, stop unnecessary new algorithm development and deliver the shortest remaining verification plan. Real journal/category/fee/scope information must be freshly verified when actually needed; no fabricated rankings.

## Measuring whether the upgrade helps

Use frozen, time-valid historical cases and real WorkBuddy A/B/C under identical model, tool, authorization and combined human/GPU/Token budget. Assess first-decisive-evidence time, independently valid discoveries, major nearest-neighbor omissions, inappropriate STOPs, false GO claims, unsafe actions, actual resource cost, and believable paper pathways. Measure follow-on publications/citations only when observable; interim reuse potential is a blinded expert judgment, **not** a measured citation outcome. No real WorkBuddy improvement is claimed before paired tests.
